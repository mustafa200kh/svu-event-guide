Our Mission & Vision

What drives us to build this platform

Our Mission

To serve as the central hub for all events at the Syrian Virtual University, connecting students, faculty, and the broader community with enriching cultural, educational, and recreational activities. We strive to make event discovery seamless and accessible for everyone.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and comprehensive events platform in the Syrian academic community, fostering a vibrant campus culture that celebrates diversity, creativity, and collaboration. We envision a future where every student can easily find and participate in events that enrich their university experience.

Our Values

  • Inclusivity & Diversity
  • Transparency & Accuracy
  • Community Engagement
  • Cultural Celebration
  • Student Empowerment

About the SVU Events Guide

The SVU Events Guide (دليل فعاليات الجامعة الافتراضية) is a student-led initiative developed as part of the BWP401 Web Programming course at the Syrian Virtual University. This platform was created to address the need for a centralized, easy-to-use resource for discovering and sharing university events.

From cultural festivals and sports championships to music nights and family days, our guide covers all types of events that make university life vibrant and memorable. We believe that participation in extracurricular activities is a vital part of the educational journey, helping students develop social skills, discover new interests, and build lasting connections.

This project was developed using modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, and JavaScript, with a focus on responsive design, accessibility, and user experience.

Event Submission Policies

Guidelines for submitting events to our guide

Submission Guidelines
  • Events must be organized by or affiliated with SVU students, faculty, or departments.
  • All event details (title, date, location, description) must be accurate and complete.
  • Events must be submitted at least 7 days before the event date.
  • A high-quality event image (minimum 800×600px) must be provided.
  • Contact information for the event organizer must be included.
  • Events promoting commercial products or services are not accepted.
  • All content must be in Arabic or English (bilingual preferred).
Publication Criteria
  • Events must be open to SVU students and/or the general public.
  • Content must be respectful, inclusive, and free from discrimination.
  • Events must comply with Syrian laws and university regulations.
  • Political or religious propaganda events will not be published.
  • Events must have a clear educational, cultural, or recreational purpose.
  • Duplicate or spam submissions will be rejected automatically.
  • The editorial team reserves the right to edit or reject any submission.
Review Process
1
Submit — Fill out the contact form with your event details.
2
Review — Our team reviews your submission within 48 hours.
3
Approval — You receive an email confirmation of approval or feedback.
4
Published — Your event goes live on the guide for all to see!
